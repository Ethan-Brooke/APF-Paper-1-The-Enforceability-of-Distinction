## Paper 1 v5.2 specific guards

### G1. Do NOT claim Paper 1 derives the Born rule
Paper 1 *previews* the Born rule (T_Born) as a downstream consequence of the noncommutative continuation algebra plus additional effect–state hypotheses; full formal development is deferred to the quantum-structure paper (Paper 5).  Claiming "Paper 1 derives the Born rule" overshoots the v5.2 scope-control posture (unchanged from v5.0).

### G2. Do NOT claim Paper 1 derives Hilbert space
Paper 1's formal endpoint is the noncommutative continuation algebra of (IJC) interfaces (T_alg).  The GNS / Wedderburn–Artin ascent to a finite-dimensional complex Hilbert representation (T2) is *previewed* and deferred to Paper 5; it requires field-selection and effect–state hypotheses not formally established here.

### G3. Do NOT claim Paper 1 derives the Tsirelson bound
Same scope-control as G1/G2.  T_Tsirelson is previewed; the 2√2 bound's full derivation requires tensor / composition identification hypotheses developed in Paper 5.

### G4. Do NOT claim "all classical models are excluded"
The precise statement is: faithful commutative Boolean-record defenders for the declared finite tested interface are excluded on the (IJC) branch.  Some classical models (Sep-branch, with admissible commuting-extension defenders) are *permitted* by PLEC; the paper does not exclude classical mechanics globally.

### G5. Do NOT claim IJC = quantum
IJC is an interface-relative algebraic verdict (no faithful commuting-extension defender exists).  Quantum systems are an empirical instance of the (IJC) branch (inherited from Bell + Kochen–Specker), not a categorical identification.  Some non-quantum (IJC) interfaces are mathematically possible.

### G6. Do NOT claim the framework "starts from a single axiom A1"
APF rests on PLEC's four constitutive features (A1, MD, A2, BW) on FD1's enforcement-interface triple — pairwise structurally independent.  A1 alone is necessary but not sufficient; the four-component framing was retired in v4.0-PLEC and restored as canonical in v4.x onward.
